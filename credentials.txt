=============================================================
               HOW TO USE EMERGENCY ACCESS
=============================================================
1. On Web Dashboard:
   - Click "Emergency Access" card.
   - Enter your account password and submit.
   - A unique 6-digit Emergency PIN will be generated with a 2-minute timer.
   - The ESP32 locker automatically detects the active Emergency PIN and
     starts the 2-minute countdown timer on its LCD screen immediately!

2. At the ESP32 Locker:
   - If you press 'A' on the keypad before generating the PIN on the web:
     The LCD will notify you: "No Active PIN! / Generate on Web".
   - Once generated, enter the 6-digit PIN on the keypad and press '#' to unlock.
   - To clear mistyped digits, press '*'. Pressing '*' on an empty PIN exits back to scan mode.
   - When verified, the relay unlocks for 4 seconds ("EMERGENCY UNLOCK").

3. Cooldown:
   - A 2-minute cooldown is enforced per user before another emergency PIN can be requested.
